# The learner and their taste

A student in bioinformatics, digital pathology and cancer genomics who uses AI/ML and learns visually. Pictures carry the story. <!-- A: audience. -->

## Learning
- Concrete example before the general rule. Draw matrices/vectors and dot products: which rows/columns are read, and the shape made. Name notation, assumptions, distribution parameters, formula origins and relatives. Explain why beside each step; ask predictions. <!-- A: audience; T: change 9. -->
- Prerequisites first; analogies with limits; a tempting shortcut compared with the answer. Class uses think stops inside the lesson, not end quizzes. <!-- C: wants/likes; D: lesson quiz. -->

## Visuals
- Bigger plots, smaller headings, little text. Deck/class: flat dark plates, square frames, thick grey borders, one orange. Sketch: paper on a dark wall. <!-- A: audience; D: look/headings/sketch. -->
- More colour coding; one colour per symbol. Short top legends; meanings in pop-ups. C10 direction: colour-matched teacher words, almost no figure text. <!-- T: Decisions; C: wants/likes. -->
- Show the changing colour/count/position; explain controls. Keep figures visible, their shape intact and formulas unclipped. Left-align text; centre figures/equations. <!-- T: Decisions 4/8; D: shape/text edges; R: 4/6. -->
- No symbol walls, invented shorthand, boxed answers or repeated boxes around text. Class formula names use pop-ups, no key row/dotted underline; glass/blur cards are an approved exception to the flat look. <!-- T: Decisions 2/3/5; D: cards; R: 2/3/5. Later decisions override old references. -->

## Working together
- Plain words. Show 2–3 options or before/after pictures, author YAML, token cost and lint result. The user chooses visually, not through diffs. Keep YAML short without losing quality. Follow the live plan's approval/testing loop. <!-- T: How we work 1/4; C: loop. -->
- Desktop/laptop checked; phones unchecked. Image-model pictures only on request; the user names the tool. Dislikes generated maths and dark label boxes over figures. <!-- A: audience; C: wants/likes. -->
